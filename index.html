Ringbloom

Turn a ring. Align three petals. Watch the garden bloom.

How to play

  1. Select the inner, middle, or outer flower ring.
  2. Swipe or tap an arrow to turn it one notch.
  3. Align the same colour and glyph across all three rings to bloom a spoke.
  4. Fill the garden meter before your moves run out.
