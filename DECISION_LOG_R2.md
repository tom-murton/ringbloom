## Prioritised improvement plan

1. **Turn choreography and input correctness:** animate the selected ring through a real 45-degree move; dwell on the solved spoke; sequence bloom/refill/outcome; serialize rapid input; use geometry-aware tangential swipes.
2. **Learnability:** highlight a guaranteed first bloom on Garden 1 and add three optional contextual hints per garden, visibly identifying both ring and direction without making the answer permanent.
3. **Mastery and fairness:** add consecutive-bloom streak scoring, remaining-move completion bonus, a three-tier garden rating, and a smoother target/move curve with a readable first board.
4. **Safe continuity:** persist the complete active engine state, make Home a pause/save path, and resume the exact board after navigation, process termination, or interruption.
5. **Accessible polish:** shorten board exploration into spoke-sized VoiceOver elements, announce turn outcomes, focus pause/outcome overlays, and ensure Reduce Motion uses the same readable sequence without sweeping motion.
6. **Store pass:** bump build, refresh the player-value metadata and required two-part description, capture gameplay-first screenshots from the final binary, upload and attach build 3, then stage a new Ready to Submit review item without triggering final submission.
