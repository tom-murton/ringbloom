Ringbloom Privacy Policy

Effective 15 August 2026

The short version

Ringbloom has no accounts or ads. It uses AppsFlyer to measure app installs, sessions and the one-time Flower Show purchase so we can understand whether our marketing works.

What we collect

AppsFlyer receives app and device information when Ringbloom opens, including app version, device model, operating system, language, network information, an AppsFlyer installation identifier, Apple’s identifier for vendors (IDFV), and an approximate location derived from the IP address. It also receives install and session activity.

After a verified purchase of Complete Flower Show, Ringbloom sends a purchase event containing the product identifier, StoreKit transaction identifier, revenue of £2.99 and currency GBP. Duplicate StoreKit updates and ordinary restores do not create another purchase event on the same installation.

How we use and share it

We use this information for attribution, campaign measurement, product analytics and fraud prevention. AppsFlyer acts as Ringbloom’s sole mobile measurement provider. Where we advertise through a partner such as TikTok, AppsFlyer may send that partner install and purchase postbacks for traffic attributed to that partner.

Ringbloom uses AppsFlyer’s Strict iOS SDK. It does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt, and does not use this information for personalised ads or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites.

What stays on your device

Your best score, highest garden, tutorial state, and sound and haptic preferences are stored only on your device. If you delete the app, iOS deletes that local data with it.

Purchases

Payment is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your card or bank details. We receive only the purchase event described above.

Children

Ringbloom does not ask for a name, age, email address or account. The AppsFlyer processing described above occurs whenever the app runs.

Service provider and retention

AppsFlyer processes this information on our behalf under its Services Privacy Policy. AppsFlyer may process data in countries outside your own and retains it according to its agreement with us and applicable law.

Your choices

You can stop future measurement by deleting Ringbloom. To ask about access, deletion or another privacy right, contact us below. Because Ringbloom has no account, we may need an AppsFlyer installation identifier to locate a record.

Changes

We will update this page before a version with materially different data practices ships.
