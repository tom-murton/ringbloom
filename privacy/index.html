Ringbloom Privacy Policy

Effective 11 July 2026

The short version

Ringbloom collects no data. None.

What we collect

Nothing. Ringbloom has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data SDKs. The app works fully offline and never sends personal data anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your best score, highest garden, tutorial state, and sound and haptic preferences are stored only on your device. If you delete the app, iOS deletes that local data with it.

Purchases

Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment details.

Children

Ringbloom collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future update changes this policy, this page will be updated before that version ships and the change will be called out in the App Store release notes.
