# Research — Ringbloom

## Recommendation

Build **Ringbloom**, a premium portrait puzzle for broad casual players who want a polished two-to-five-minute session. Players rotate three concentric flower rings one notch at a time. When three matching petals line up on a spoke, they bloom, clear, and refill. The interaction is immediately legible, visually ownable, and small enough to implement and verify in one session.

## Originality and feasibility

An exact-name web and App Store search found no game titled “Ringbloom” on 11 July 2026. Search did find unrelated uses of “Ring Bloom,” so final title acceptance remains subject to the human app-record creation step. The radial three-ring matching mechanic, petal/glyph language, scoring rules, and level generator are original to this run.

The game needs no backend, account, ads, IAP, authored level pack, physics tuning, or licensed IP. Native SwiftUI animation and procedural vector shapes cover the whole playable surface; generated audio and icon art are the only shipped external assets.
